# Easy Video Downloader – User Guide

Easy Video Downloader saves videos or music from a web link onto your Windows PC.
You paste a link, choose Video or Audio, and press **Download**. That's it.

---

## What you need

- A Windows 10 or Windows 11 PC
- An internet connection
- About 300 MB of free disk space (plus room for your downloads)

You do **not** need to install anything else.

---

## 1. Get the program

1. Click this link:
   **[Download Easy Video Downloader](https://github.com/aksahoo919-ui/video-downloader/releases/latest/download/Easy-Video-Downloader.exe)**
2. Your browser saves a file called **Easy-Video-Downloader.exe**, usually in your **Downloads** folder.
3. *(Optional)* Move it to your Desktop so it's easy to find.

There is no installer. The file you downloaded **is** the program.

### If your browser warns you

Some browsers say the file "isn't commonly downloaded". Choose **Keep** (in Edge: click the **…** next to the
download, then **Keep**, then **Keep anyway**).

---

## 2. Open the program for the first time

1. Double-click **Easy-Video-Downloader.exe**.
2. Windows may show a blue box saying **"Windows protected your PC"**.
   This appears because the program is new and not signed by a big company. It is expected.
   - Click **More info**
   - Then click **Run anyway**
3. The first time, the program downloads the tools it needs to work (about 260 MB).
   This can take a few minutes depending on your internet speed. Please wait until it says it is ready.

After this first time, the program opens quickly.

---

## 3. Download a video or song

The window has three simple steps.

### Step 1 – Paste the video link

1. In your browser, open the video you want.
2. Copy the address from the address bar at the top (click it, then press **Ctrl + C**).
3. In Easy Video Downloader, click **Paste link**.

**Several videos at once?** Put one link on each line.
Use **Clear** to empty the box.

### Step 2 – Choose what you want

- **Video** – saves the video with sound.
- **Audio only (music)** – saves just the sound, for example as an MP3.

Then pick a **Quality**:

| If you chose Video | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Best available | Highest quality the site offers (largest file) |
| 4K / 2K / Full HD / HD | Sharp picture, bigger files |
| Standard (480p) / Small file (360p) | Smaller files, good for phones |

| If you chose Audio | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| MP3 – Best / Good / Small file | Works on every phone, car and player. **Good** is fine for most people. |
| M4A – Original quality | Same quality as the original, small file |
| FLAC – Lossless | Perfect quality, large file |
| WAV – Uncompressed | Very large files, for editing |
| OGG | Smaller files, works in most modern players |

The program remembers your choice for next time.

### Step 3 – Choose where to save

Files are saved in **Downloads → Video Downloads** unless you click **Change…** and pick another folder.
The program remembers the folder you picked.

### Press Download

Click the big red **Download** button. You'll see progress underneath.

- To cancel, click **Stop**.
- When it's done, click **Open download folder** to see your file.

---

## Updates

You don't need to do anything. Each time you open the program, it checks for updates to its download
tools and installs them by itself. This keeps downloads working when websites change.

You can also click **Check for updates** at the bottom of the window at any time.

To get a newer version of the program itself, just download it again from the link in step 1 and replace
the old file.

---

## Something went wrong?

The program tells you in plain words what happened. Common messages:

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Could not reach the internet | Check your Wi-Fi or cable and try again. |
| That link did not work / does not look like a video link | Go back to the video, copy the link again, and paste it. |
| This video is private / members only / age-restricted | These videos can't be downloaded. |
| This video is unavailable / not available in your country | The video was removed or is blocked where you are. |
| The disk is full | Delete some files or choose another folder in Step 3. |
| Windows did not allow saving to that folder | Click **Change…** and pick a different folder, such as Documents. |
| YouTube is temporarily limiting downloads | Wait a while (an hour or so) and try again. |

### Other tips

- **First start shows "Try again":** the tool download was interrupted. Check your internet and click **Try again**.
- **Downloads suddenly stop working:** click **Check for updates**, then try again.
- **Want to see exactly what happened?** Click **Show details**.
- **Start completely fresh:** close the program, press **Windows key + R**, type
  `%LOCALAPPDATA%\EasyVideoDownloader`, press Enter, and delete everything in that folder.
  The next start will set things up again.

---

## Removing the program

1. Delete **Easy-Video-Downloader.exe**.
2. *(Optional)* Delete the folder `%LOCALAPPDATA%\EasyVideoDownloader` (see "Start completely fresh" above) to remove its tools and settings.

Your downloaded videos and music are not affected.

---
